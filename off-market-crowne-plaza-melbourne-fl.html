The Asset

An oceanfront box on Florida's most under-priced coastline.

Sited on the Indialantic barrier island directly off Highway A1A, the Crowne Plaza Melbourne–Oceanfront occupies one of the few large-format oceanfront parcels remaining in Brevard County. The asset trades on scale (290 keys), location (direct Atlantic frontage), and a current select-upscale flag that meaningfully under-monetizes the dirt.

Indialantic sits inside the Palm Bay–Melbourne–Titusville MSA — the operating heart of America's commercial space industry, anchored by Kennedy Space Center, SpaceX, Blue Origin, L3Harris, and Northrop Grumman. Demand drivers are growing while branded supply on the sand is structurally constrained.

Strategic Fit

Three reasons this asset matches the brief you gave us.

01

Inside the Buy-Box

Your stated criteria — "hotels of 100-plus rooms, on or a couple blocks off the ocean" — describes this asset almost word for word. 290 keys with direct Atlantic frontage on a barrier island. No interpretation required.

02

Conversion Upside

A tired Crowne Plaza on irreplaceable oceanfront dirt is a textbook flag-down, brand-up trade. Stripping the IHG flag and repositioning to a Viceroy lifestyle product captures rate, margin, and identity that the current operator is structurally unable to access.

03

Demand You Can’t Engineer

Brevard’s commercial-space economy is producing weekday corporate compression that traditional Florida leisure markets simply don’t see. Kennedy, SpaceX, Blue Origin, L3Harris, and Patrick Space Force Base are all within 45 minutes — a year-round demand floor under a beachfront luxury asset.
