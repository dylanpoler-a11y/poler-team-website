### Creative Framework — Listing-Specific Ads

**Each ad MUST feature:**
1. A specific property with real listing photos
2. The actual price (e.g., "$3,000,000")
3. Key property details (beds, baths, sqft, waterfront)
4. Location (Fort Lauderdale, Miami Beach, etc.)
5. MLS number for credibility

**Headline formula (Spanish/LATAM):**
- "Casa de Lujo en Fort Lauderdale – $3,000,000"
- "Propiedad Frente al Mar – $2,500,000 – 4 Hab / 3 Banos"
- "Mansion en Miami Beach – Vista al Oceano – $4,200,000"

**Headline formula (English/USA):**
- "Waterfront Estate – Fort Lauderdale – $3,000,000"
- "Luxury Home on the Water – 4BD/3BA – $2,500,000"
- "Oceanfront Living – Miami Beach – $4,200,000"

**Body copy formula:**
1. Lead with the property's best feature (waterfront, ocean view, new construction)
2. Key stats: beds, baths, sqft, lot size
3. Location and lifestyle appeal
4. Low-commitment CTA: "See full details" / "Schedule a private showing"

**Image rules:**
- **Full exterior shots showing the complete house** — this is what's converting at $2.82 CPL
- The winning photo shows the full property from the front/street level, not aerial
- Luxury curb appeal matters — the home should look impressive and aspirational
- Exterior shots showing water/pool/landscaping > interior-only shots
- Bright, sunlit photos with green landscaping and blue sky
- NO text overlays on images (Meta penalizes these)
- Carousel ads with 3-5 best listing photos can be tested against single image
- Do NOT assume aerial/drone shots work best — test them, but the proven winner is a ground-level full exterior

### Listing Selection Criteria

When choosing which listings to advertise, prioritize:
1. **Waterfront/oceanfront** — these consistently get the lowest CPL
2. **Price range $2M–$5M** — sweet spot for serious international and domestic buyers
3. **Fort Lauderdale area** — proven best-performing market
4. **Great photos available** — the ad is only as good as the photo
5. **Active listings** — don't advertise sold or pending properties

### Optimization Cadence

**Daily:**
- Check CPL across all active ads
- Flag any ad spending over $5 with zero leads

**Every 3 days:**
- Compare ad-level performance within each ad set
- Pause any ad with CPL 3x above target after $15+ spend

**Every 5-7 days:**
- Kill lowest performing ad in each ad set
- Swap in a NEW listing as challenger against the winner
- The winner stays until beaten or creative fatigues

**Every 2 weeks:**
- Evaluate ad set-level performance
- Adjust budgets: increase on winners, decrease on losers
- Test new audience segments or lookalikes
- Check if winning listing is still active (not sold/pending)

**Monthly:**
- Full campaign audit
- Rotate in fresh listings across all ad sets
- Evaluate lead quality with Kevin (are leads converting to appointments?)
- Review total spend vs leads generated vs deals closed

### A/B Testing Protocol
1. Test ONE variable at a time: listing photo, headline, body copy, or audience
2. Minimum 5 days or $30 spend before calling a winner
3. Winner = lowest CPL with at least 3+ leads
4. Document every test result to build compounding knowledge
5. Always have a challenger listing running against the champion
6. When testing new listings: use the proven ad copy formula, only change the property

## Common Issues

### CPL too high on Meta
1. Is the listing waterfront with great photos? Swap if not.
2. Are you using lead forms? (Lead forms always beat website traffic)
3. Check frequency — above 3.0 means audience fatigue, swap listing
4. Check ad age — listing ads over 2 weeks old need refresh
5. Try a different price point listing ($2M-$5M sweet spot)

### Zero conversions on Google
1. Verify conversion tracking tag is firing on the landing page
2. Check search terms for irrelevant queries — add negatives
3. Check landing page load speed
4. Check ad relevance to keywords
5. Is budget too low to exit learning phase? ($10/day may be tight)

### Lead quality is poor
1. Add price in the ad to pre-qualify ("$3,000,000 Waterfront Estate")
2. Add qualifying question to lead form ("Budget range?")
3. Tighten audience targeting (income, interests)
4. For Google: more negative keywords to filter browsers
5. Review which specific listings produce leads that convert to showings
