# Google Ads for Real Estate — Eric Preston Proven System

Based on the exact methodology from Eric Preston, who has personally spent $10M+ on Google Ads for real estate agents and built one of the largest Google Ads agencies in real estate. This is NOT theory — this is battle-tested over 7+ years.

## CRITICAL RULES (Do NOT Violate)

1. **SEARCH ADS ONLY** — Never use Performance Max, Display, or any other campaign type. Search is where the highest-quality leads come from.
2. **DO NOT use AI Max for Search Campaigns** — Google's AI will override your optimized settings. Skip keyword and asset generation.
3. **DO NOT trust Google Reps** — They are generalists, not real estate experts. Their "free optimization" calls have 10x'd client CPLs from $15 to $150. Ignore their emails.
4. **BROAD MATCH + NEGATIVE KEYWORDS** — Use broad match keywords corrected by an extensive negative keyword list. This is the proven system.
5. **SEARCH CONTINUITY** — The search term, ad headline, and landing page MUST all align. If someone searches "Miami homes for sale", the ad should say "Miami Homes for Sale" and land on a Miami-filtered property page.
6. **LIST VIEW, SORTED BY NEWEST** — Landing pages must show list view (NOT grid, NOT map) sorted by newest listings (NOT highest price). This is proven to convert best.
7. **PIN YOUR HEADLINES** — Always pin headlines to their positions. Unpin = Google may show "Live MLS Search" first when someone searched "Dallas Homes for Sale" = broken search continuity.

## Campaign Architecture

```
CAMPAIGN (Search) — "Poler Team Google Leads" or similar
  Budget: $30-$50/day ($500-1500/month)
  Bidding: Maximize Conversions (no target CPA)
  Networks: Google Search ONLY (deselect Search Partners + Display Network)

  AD GROUP 1: [City/Area Name] — e.g., "Miami Beach"
    Keywords: Broad match city-specific terms
    Ad: Headlines + descriptions aligned to that city/area
    Landing URL: Filtered to that area, list view, sorted newest

  AD GROUP 2: [Another City/Area] — e.g., "Brickell"
    Keywords: Broad match for Brickell
    Ad: Headlines aligned to Brickell
    Landing URL: Filtered to Brickell, list view, sorted newest

  AD GROUP 3: [Niche] — e.g., "Miami Condos"
    Keywords: Condo-specific broad match
    Ad: Headlines say "Condo Search" not "Property Search"
    Landing URL: Filtered to condos only

  NEGATIVE KEYWORDS (Campaign Level) — applies to ALL ad groups
```

### Budget Guidelines
- **$30-$50/day** recommended ($900-$1,500/month)
- **$300-$500/month per area** is a good benchmark
- More areas does NOT mean more leads — it spreads spend thinner
- Don't always target the hottest/most expensive areas (most competitive = highest CPCs)
- Target areas with high search volume that you actually service
- Up-and-coming areas can be goldmines with lower competition

## Location Targeting Setup

### Three-Level Location Structure
Always include THREE location levels so you can later see where leads originate:

1. **Country level** — United States
2. **State level** — Florida
3. **City level** — Miami (or specific target city)

### CRITICAL: Location Options
Click the location options dropdown and change to:
- **Presence** (people IN or REGULARLY IN your targeted location)
- Do NOT use "Presence or interest" (default) — this shows your ad to people in India searching Miami properties

This captures relocation leads (someone in California searching for Miami homes) while keeping geographic relevance.

### Languages
- English (always)
- Add Spanish if you serve Spanish-speaking clients
- Add other languages as relevant to your market

## Audience Segments (In-Market)

Search for "homes for sale" and select these IN-MARKET audiences:
- Houses for sale
- New houses for sale
- Residential properties for sale
- Apartments for sale
- New apartments for sale
- Homeowners

**SKIP all "rent" audiences.**

### Audience Setting: OBSERVATION (not Targeting)
Leave the setting as "Observation" — this tells Google to observe if these in-market audiences convert better, and if they do, spend more budget on them. Do NOT set to "Targeting" as Google's data isn't perfect enough to restrict to only these audiences.

## Ad Copy Template

### Final URL Construction
1. Go to your website
2. Search for the target area
3. Switch to **LIST VIEW**
4. Sort by **NEWEST** (not highest price)
5. Copy that URL
6. Add UTM tracking: `&utm_source=google_ad`

Example: `https://homesinsoflorida.com/listing?area=miami-beach&sort=newest&view=list&utm_source=google_ad`

### Display Path
- If your domain does NOT contain "search": add `/search/[city]`
- If your domain contains "search": just add `/[city]`
- The word "search" in the display path improves CTR because it aligns with search intent

Example: `homesinsoflorida.com/search/miami-beach`

### Headlines (15 total, PINNED to positions)

**Pin Position 1 — City-First Headlines (always show first):**
1. `[City] [State] Property Search`
2. `[City] [State] Homes for Sale`
3. `[City] Real Estate Search`
4. `{KeyWord:City Homes for Sale}` — Dynamic keyword insertion

**Pin Position 2 — Complementary/Value Headlines:**
5. `Live MLS Search`
6. `Updated Listings 24/7`
7. `[#,###] Active Listings` — Replace with actual count from your site
8. `Search Active Listings [City]`
9. `Award-Winning Property Search`
10. `Fast & Free Real Estate Search`

**Pin Position 3 — Additional Value Props:**
11. `Browse All [City] Listings`
12. `Find Your Dream Home Today`
13. `New Listings Added Daily`
14. `Search by Price, Size & More`
15. `Start Your Home Search Now`

### Descriptions (4 total)
1. `Search all [City], [State] listings updated in real-time. Browse homes, condos, and properties with photos, prices, and details. Start your search today.`
2. `Find your perfect home in [City]. Our comprehensive MLS search shows every available property. Updated 24/7 with the latest listings.`
3. `Looking for homes in [City], [State]? Search thousands of active listings by price, bedrooms, and neighborhood. Free, fast, and always up to date.`
4. `Explore [City] real estate with our powerful property search. New listings added daily. See photos, virtual tours, and property details instantly.`

### Dynamic Keyword Insertion
Use `{KeyWord:Default Text}` syntax in one Pin 1 headline. This dynamically inserts the exact search term as the headline, creating perfect search continuity.

Example: If someone searches "Miami Beach apartments for sale", the headline becomes "Miami Beach Apartments for Sale"

## Site Links (Optional but Recommended)
Add sub-areas within your target city as site links:
- Each site link = a neighborhood/sub-area
- Each links to a filtered search page showing ONLY that sub-area
- Must be list view, sorted newest

Example for Miami:
- Brickell → filtered to Brickell
- South Beach → filtered to South Beach
- Coral Gables → filtered to Coral Gables

## Callouts
Add these callout extensions:
- Upsizers
- Downsizers
- Relocation
- First-Time Buyers

These are words that "call out" to specific buyer types and increase CTR.

## Conversion Tracking Setup

### Conversion Action Settings
- **Category:** Sign Up
- **Conversion Name:** `[domain] Lead Form Submit`
- **Value:** Don't use a value
- **Count:** ONE (not "Every") — counts one lead per person, not multiple form fills
- **Enhanced Conversions:** Turn ON

### Two Pieces of Code Required
1. **Google Global Site Tag (gtag.js)** — installed on EVERY page in `<head>`
2. **Event Snippet** — fires when someone becomes a lead (form submission)

## Optimization Checklist (Ongoing)

### Weekly Tasks
- [ ] Review Search Terms report — add irrelevant terms to negative keyword list
- [ ] Check which ad groups are getting clicks vs. conversions
- [ ] Pause underperforming ad groups
- [ ] Increase budget on top performers

### Monthly Tasks
- [ ] Review device performance (desktop vs. mobile) — shift budget to winner
- [ ] Update active listing counts in headlines
- [ ] Refresh ad copy if CTR is declining
- [ ] Review location data — where are leads actually coming from?
- [ ] Check competitive landscape — are CPCs rising in certain areas?

### Red Flags to Watch
- CPL above $20 consistently → review keywords and negatives
- High impressions, low CTR → ad copy doesn't match search intent
- High clicks, no conversions → landing page issue or wrong traffic
- "Other search terms" eating >30% of budget → need more negative keywords
- Ads showing in wrong geographies → check location settings (Presence vs. Interest)

## Campaign Duplication for Multiple Areas
1. Select the ad group you want to duplicate
2. Edit → Copy → Paste into same campaign
3. Rename the new ad group to the new area
4. Update ALL keywords to reference the new area
5. Update ALL headlines to reference the new area
6. Update the Final URL to filter for the new area
7. Update the Display Path
8. Ensure search continuity: keyword → headline → landing page ALL match

## What NOT to Do
- Do NOT use Performance Max
- Do NOT enable Google Search Partners
- Do NOT enable Google Display Network
- Do NOT use AI Max for Search Campaigns
- Do NOT let Google reps "optimize" your campaigns
- Do NOT set a target CPA (let Maximize Conversions learn)
- Do NOT use grid or map view on landing pages
- Do NOT sort landing pages by highest price
- Do NOT target luxury keywords (low conversion)
- Do NOT forget to pin headlines (breaks search continuity)
- Do NOT add negative keywords at ad group level unless specific reason (use campaign level)
