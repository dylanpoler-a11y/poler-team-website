## Posting Strategy (3x/week)

### Post Types to Rotate

**1. Insight Post** (most powerful for authority)
Share a real lesson from an actual deal, campaign, or client situation. No filler.

Format:
```
[Strong first line that makes them stop scrolling — a counterintuitive truth, a specific number, or a short story opening]

[2-4 short paragraphs with the actual insight. Specific details. No vague advice.]

[One-line takeaway]
```

Examples of strong first lines:
- "Got a $0.44 cost per lead on a Facebook campaign last month. Here's exactly how."
- "Most investors I talk to are analyzing deals wrong. Here's what they miss."
- "I've closed deals where the numbers looked terrible on paper. Here's why we still bought."

**2. Market Observation Post**
Short, punchy take on what's happening in South Florida right now. Data-backed when possible.

Format:
```
[One sharp observation about the market]

[2-3 sentences with the evidence or logic]

[What this means for buyers/sellers/investors right now]
```

**3. Behind-the-Scenes Post**
Real thing Kevin is working on — a campaign he's running, a deal being underwritten, a client situation (anonymized). Makes him human and credible.

Format:
```
[What he's working on right now — specific]

[What the challenge is]

[What he's trying / what he's learned so far]

[Open question to the audience]
```

**4. Service-Adjacent Post** (max 1 per 3 posts — never salesy)
Teach something useful that happens to relate to one of Kevin's Fiverr services.

Examples:
- "How I underwrite a deal before going under contract" (→ deal underwriting gig)
- "The Facebook ad copy formula that gets real estate leads under $2" (→ ad copy gig)
- "What a real estate business plan actually needs to raise private money" (→ business plan gig)

Format: Same as insight post. No pitch at the end — just value. If someone asks "can you help with this?" respond naturally.

## Engagement Strategy — Finding & Starting Conversations

### Where to Engage

1. **Search for posts about:** real estate investing, South Florida market, Facebook ads for realtors, lead generation, deal analysis, multifamily, short-term rentals, raising capital
2. **Target profiles:** Real estate investors, real estate agents, syndicators, property managers, mortgage brokers, developers — especially those posting about deals or challenges
3. **Prioritize posts with:** 10-200 comments (active conversation, not too crowded), posted within 48 hours, genuine question or strong opinion

### How to Engage (The Kevin Approach)

**Never:** Generic "Great post!" / "This is so true!" / "Thanks for sharing!"

**Always:** Add something real — a different angle, a data point, a specific experience, a respectful pushback, or a genuine question.

**Strong comment templates:**

When someone shares a deal or market take:
→ "The [specific detail they mentioned] is exactly what we're seeing in South Florida too — [1 sentence of your actual experience]. One thing I'd add: [your insight]. What's driving that on your end?"

When someone asks a question you can answer:
→ Answer it directly and specifically. Don't hedge. Don't sell. Just answer it like the most helpful person in the room. End with: "Happy to dig deeper on this if useful."

When someone shares a problem you've solved:
→ "[I've / We've] dealt with exactly this. What worked for us was [specific thing]. The mistake most people make is [specific mistake]. Did you try [specific approach]?"

When you disagree:
→ "Respectfully, [the thing you disagree with] usually breaks down when [specific scenario]. In my experience [your actual experience]. What's your take on [the nuance]?"

### Relationship Progression

**Stage 1 — First comment:** Leave a substantive comment on their post. No DM yet.
**Stage 2 — Follow-up:** If they respond to your comment, continue the conversation naturally in the thread. Show you read what they wrote.
**Stage 3 — DM (only after genuine thread interaction):** Keep it short and specific. Reference the conversation. No pitch.
  → "Hey [name] — good thread earlier on [topic]. I work primarily in South Florida but [something specific you noticed about their situation]. Would be good to connect if you're ever evaluating deals or lead gen in that market."
**Stage 4 — Relationship:** Share relevant content, make introductions, engage on their future posts. Build the relationship before any transaction.

## People Skills Framework

### Reading the Person

- **Analytical/numbers person:** Lead with data and specific metrics. Skip the story.
- **Vision/big picture person:** Lead with the opportunity or market dynamic. Then get specific.
- **Skeptical/experienced:** Acknowledge what they know first, then add your layer. Don't over-explain.
- **New/learning:** Be generous with specifics. Don't condescend. Share what you wish you knew.
- **Already successful:** Respect their time. Be peer-to-peer, not fan-to-idol.

### Building Trust Quickly on LinkedIn

1. **Be specific.** Vague advice is forgettable. Specific experience is memorable.
2. **Don't chase validation.** Post what's true, not what'll get the most likes.
3. **Engage consistently with the same people** over weeks, not just once.
4. **Be honest about what you don't know.** It builds more credibility than pretending.
5. **Show up in the comments before you show up in the DMs.**

### Sales Skills (when the time comes)

Never sell in a comment or first DM. The sale happens when:
- They've seen your perspective multiple times and trust it
- They have a specific problem you can solve
- They reach out to YOU, or the conversation naturally moves there

When it does:
- Ask the one question that unlocks the scope: "What's the actual problem you're trying to solve?"
- Listen more than you talk
- Match the package to their situation, not the other way around
- Make it easy: "Here's what I'd do for your specific situation..."
- Close simply: "Want me to put together a custom offer based on what you've described?"

## What NOT to Do

- No motivational content that could come from anyone ("Success is a mindset!")
- No engagement bait ("Comment YES if you agree!")
- No copy-paste compliments on posts
- No pitching services in comments or first DMs
- No posting more than once per day
- No tagging people who weren't in the conversation
- No vanity metrics chasing — one real connection > 100 empty likes
