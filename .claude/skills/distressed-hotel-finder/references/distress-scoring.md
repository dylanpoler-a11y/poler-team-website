# Distress Scoring Reference

## Point Values by Category

### Financial Distress

| Indicator | Points | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| 1 year tax delinquent | 3 | Check county tax collector |
| 2+ years tax delinquent | 5 | Tax deed application possible |
| Tax certificate sold | 7 | Imminent risk of losing property |
| Active lis pendens | 8 | Lawsuit filed against property |
| Foreclosure in progress | 9 | Lender pursuing seizure |
| Scheduled for auction | 10 | Maximum urgency |
| Active liens (mechanic, judgment) | 3-5 | Based on amount relative to value |

### Code & Physical Condition

| Indicator | Points | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| 1-2 open code violations | 2 | Minor issues |
| 3-5 open code violations | 4 | Pattern of neglect |
| 6+ open violations | 6 | Serious neglect |
| Fire code or structural violations | 7 | Safety hazard — may force closure |
| Expired permits (renovation started, not finished) | 3 | Abandoned improvement project |
| Health department violations | 5 | Particularly relevant for hotels |

### Owner Life Events

| Indicator | Points | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Owner deceased (probate filing) | 8 | Heirs often want quick sale |
| Divorce filing involving owner | 6 | Court may order sale |
| Out-of-state owner | 3 | Less engaged management |
| Out-of-country owner | 5 | Often willing to sell at discount |
| Elderly individual owner (not entity) | 3 | Potential estate planning motivation |
| Multiple properties in distress (same owner) | 4 | Overwhelmed owner |

### Listing & Market Signals

| Indicator | Points | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| 90-179 days on market | 2 | Getting stale |
| 180+ days on market | 4 | Significant market rejection |
| 1 price reduction | 2 | Adjusting expectations |
| 2+ price reductions | 4 | Increasingly motivated |
| Price/key well below market | 3 | Already discounted |
| "As-is," "motivated seller," "value-add" in description | 3 | Seller signaling willingness |
| Listed then delisted then relisted | 4 | Failed sale attempt |

### Reputation & Operations

| Indicator | Points | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Google/TripAdvisor rating below 2.0 | 7 | Severely damaged reputation |
| Rating 2.0-3.0 | 5 | Poor operations |
| Rating 3.0-3.5 with declining trend | 3 | Deteriorating |
| No reviews in 6+ months | 4 | Possible closure |
| Keywords: "dirty," "roaches," "mold," "unsafe" | 5 | Health/safety concerns |
| Keywords: "closed," "abandoned," "renovation" | 8 | Possible non-operational |
| Hotel rooms listed on Airbnb/VRBO | 3 | Channel desperation |
| Below-market STR rates | 5 | Revenue distress |

## Multipliers

Apply after summing base points:

| Condition | Multiplier |
|-----------|-----------|
| 3+ different distress categories | 1.25x |
| Financial distress AND operational distress | 1.5x |
| Foreclosure + bad reviews + code violations ("perfect storm") | Flag as TOP PRIORITY |

## Classification

| Score Range | Classification | Action |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| 0-5 | Low Distress | Monitor only — add to watchlist |
| 6-15 | Moderate Distress | Worth investigating — gather more data |
| 16-25 | High Distress | Strong target — initiate outreach |
| 26+ | Severe Distress | Urgent opportunity — immediate action |

## Recommended Approach by Distress Type

### Foreclosure / Lis Pendens
- Contact lender's asset manager or special servicer
- Prepare all-cash offer with proof of funds
- Offer quick close (14-21 days)
- Consider purchasing the note if pre-foreclosure

### Probate / Owner Death
- Contact estate attorney (search probate case for attorney of record)
- Offer to purchase "as-is" with quick close
- Be patient but persistent — probate can take months
- Heirs often prefer cash over managing a hotel

### Divorce
- Contact both parties' attorneys
- Court may order property sale — position as a buyer
- Quick close is attractive to both parties wanting to move on

### Tax Delinquency
- Consider tax deed purchase path (buy the tax certificate)
- Direct outreach to owner offering to solve their tax problem
- Include back taxes in your offer price

### Operational Distress (Bad Reviews, Low Occupancy)
- Direct mail campaign to owner
- Offer management takeover or purchase
- Present a turnaround plan to demonstrate value-add potential
- Owner may be exhausted and ready to exit

### Out-of-State/Country Owner
- Skip trace for contact info (PropertyShark People Search or other)
- Direct outreach — they may not realize what the property is worth
- Offer to handle everything — inspections, closing, etc.
