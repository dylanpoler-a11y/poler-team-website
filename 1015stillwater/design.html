MODEL NOTES

From the
architectural plans.

This version uses the client-supplied 31-sheet architectural set labeled Revision 8, together with the two client renderings and 42 construction photographs.

What has been revised

The tapered site, building outlines, two open-to-sky courts, floor elevations, room layout, stairs, elevator, roof and waterfront pool are modeled from the drawings. Annotated dimensions control the geometry; details without dimensions are traced to the printed scale.

Explore the layout

Choose a floor to see a sectional view with room labels. Walls are cut down for visibility in these views; the whole-home view and downloaded model retain their full height.

Finished interior concepts

Warm contemporary, Bright coastal and Dramatic luxury are switchable furnishing schemes. Travertine floors, a lighter kitchen, wider wood stair treads and frameless stair glass follow the client’s latest direction and remain consistent across all three schemes. Stair glass has metal only at its floor or tread mounting. Exact tread dimensions, stone products and cabinet colours remain visual approximations. Furniture and decorative lighting are proposed staging; vanity timber, charcoal basins and pale bath stone are guided by the construction references.

What remains illustrative

This is a plan-based presentation model, not a surveyed as-built or a construction BIM file. Furniture, small fittings, structural details, landscaping and final finishes are illustrative or simplified. The drawings may differ from later site changes shown in the photographs. The original architectural PDF remains private.
