Two properties, one operation, exponential upside

Hidden Upside Within 505

Before you touch 506 — Lot 505 alone is already a development site

The 505 assemblage sits on three contiguous lots totaling 21,450 SF. Today, only 6,869 SF of building sits on that land — roughly a third of the lot is unbuilt "lazy equity." Lot 20 (the 505 folio itself) houses the existing structure plus asphalt overflow parking, leaving ample room for vertical expansion or amenities. RO zoning permits a 5-story vertical build (55 ft height), and the adjacent Avalon at 410 SE 16th Ct — an 8-story, 243-unit luxury apartment with Whole Foods on the ground floor, built 2020 — already validates this corridor for institutional density.
