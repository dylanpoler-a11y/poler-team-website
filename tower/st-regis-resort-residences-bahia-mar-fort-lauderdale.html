About St. Regis Resort & Residences, Bahia Mar Fort Lauderdale

A $1 billion, nearly 40-acre transformation of the historic Bahia Mar marina, with two 23-story residential towers alongside a 24-story resort tower and a 30-story hotel. Three- and four-bedroom homes feature private elevator foyers and wraparound terraces overlooking the Atlantic, Intracoastal, and Fort Lauderdale skyline.
