About The Avenue Coral Gables

The Avenue is a 12-story, 54-residence hotel-condo hybrid and Coral Gables' first tower approved for short-term rentals, giving owners hotel-style flexibility in the heart of the Gables. Compact one- and two-bedroom layouts come fully furnished with European kitchens by Adriana Hoyos and Sub-Zero/Wolf appliances.
