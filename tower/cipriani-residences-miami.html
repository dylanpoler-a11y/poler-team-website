About Cipriani Residences Miami

Mast Capital's Cipriani Residences is Arquitectonica's curved, 80-story tower on the Miami River in Brickell, the tallest residential building south of New York and more than 75% sold. Interiors by 1508 London bring genuine Cipriani hospitality service to full-floor and penthouse residences with sweeping bay, river, and skyline views. It is the first Cipriani-branded residential tower in the world.
