About Waldorf Astoria Residences Miami

At 1,049 feet and 100 stories, Waldorf Astoria Hotel & Residences Miami is the city's first supertall skyscraper — nine staggered glass cubes designed with Carlos Ott and Sieger Suarez, interiors by BAMO. 360 condo residences sit above a 205-key Waldorf Astoria hotel, with construction past the 50th floor.
