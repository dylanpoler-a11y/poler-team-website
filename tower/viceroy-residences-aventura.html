About Viceroy Residences Aventura

Viceroy Residences Aventura brings the hospitality brand's hotel-inspired lifestyle to a 28-story tower of 274 residences designed by Arquitectonica with interiors by DesignAgency. Floor-to-ceiling windows, expansive terraces, and Miele kitchens define the one- to four-bedroom layouts, with a resort-scale amenity deck and rooftop lounge.
