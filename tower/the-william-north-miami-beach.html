About The William North Miami Beach

A 26-story, 374-residence tower by Carlos Ott with Behar Font & Partners, The William reinterprets North Miami Beach's midcentury heritage with a louvered facade and 40,000+ sq ft of amenities across two sky decks. Studio-to-three-bedroom layouts with interiors by Urban Robot are priced to capture entry-level luxury buyers.
