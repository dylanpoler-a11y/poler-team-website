About The Perigon Miami Beach

An 18-story, 83-residence oceanfront tower in mid-Beach designed by Rem Koolhaas's OMA, one of the most architecturally significant new towers on the barrier island. Interiors by Tara Bernerd & Partners with custom Italian kitchens. Well advanced, selling remaining inventory and penthouses.
