About The Residences at 1428 Brickell

The Residences at 1428 Brickell is an ultra-exclusive bayfront tower by Ytech — just 189 residences across 70 stories, designed by ACPV and Arquitectonica. As the world's first partially solar-powered high-rise, it pairs column-free 40-ft spans and 11+ ft ceilings with 80,000 sq ft of amenities and a rooftop observatory on floor 70.
