About Shore Club Private Collection

A reimagining of the landmark Art Deco Shore Club by Witkoff and Robert A.M. Stern, spread across a rare 3-acre oceanfront estate in South Beach. Only 49 residences split between an 18-story tower, the boutique Cromwell House, and a single Beach House, all serviced by Auberge Resorts Collection.
