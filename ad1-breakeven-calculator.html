How the math works

Per HMA economics: Each closed deal generates the annual AD1 fee every year for the life of the HMA. Poler's bonus is paid only in the first 3 years: 20% · 10% · 5% of the annual fee. After year 3, the full fee is AD1's forever.

Net to AD1 per HMA over horizon H: Year 1 = 80% · Year 2 = 90% · Year 3 = 95% · Years 4 through H = 100% of the annual fee.

Breakeven test: AD1 breaks even on the hourly when net HMA fees earned (after bonus) exceed total hourly paid. The breakeven panel shows how many deals at the current fee size cover the hourly investment.
