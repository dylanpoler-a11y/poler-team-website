The Community

Built around a lake. Designed for how you live now.

Via Ventura is a master-planned, gated community developed by Lennar on former golf-course land in North Miami Beach. The community is built around a sparkling private lake, with three distinct neighborhoods — Las Ramblas, Paseos, and Vistas — sharing a clubhouse, resort-style pool, fitness center, parks, and walking trails.

Every home is a two-story contemporary townhome or villa with open floor plans, high ceilings, energy-efficient windows, and smart-home technology standard (Alexa voice control, Wi-Fi thermostat, Ring doorbell). The community is sold out — no new construction inventory — making resales the only way in.

With 24/7 on-site security, a tight 0.4% vacancy rate, and the Aventura lifestyle minutes away, demand here outpaces supply consistently.

The Three Neighborhoods

Las Ramblas, Paseos, and Vistas

Each neighborhood has its own sub-association, slightly different floor plans, and a distinct character — all under the Via Ventura master HOA.
