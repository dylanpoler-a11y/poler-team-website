Methodology

Why our numbers do not look like a banker's.

  1. 01

    Filter at intake

    Per-target ownership research before any outreach. PE-backed, public, and big-chain operators get filtered out before a single call. Trivest's investment thesis sits inside our default filter.

  2. 02

    Multi-touch sequencing

    Phone, voicemail, LinkedIn, text. Single-channel campaigns die at five percent response. The 33.7% number is what compounds across channels.

  3. 03

    Live CRM, not PDF reports

    You log into the Airtable dashboard. Pipeline is visible in real time. Weekly reporting is for review, not for status.

  4. 04

    Weekly buyer call

    Criteria gets retuned every seven days. New geographies, new size bands, new exclusions. The longer we run, the sharper the targeting.

  5. 05

    Human delivery, AI-augmented research

    Every call is from a Poler. Every research note is human-reviewed. AI is in the prep, not the conversation.
