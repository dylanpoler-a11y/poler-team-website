What's driving the lift:
• Ancillary revenue capture up +54% 2024→2025 — cleaning fees +48% and service charges +58% as the dynamic-pricing engine and integrated tech stack push more revenue per stay.
• Janitorial -14%, supplies -18%, R&M -15% 2024→2025 — tech-enabled operations + the part-time onsite manager model are showing in the cost structure.
• Revenue trajectory: rolling T12 has stabilized in the $730-750K band as the property matured into its post-PIP operating profile — operator's audited T12 (Apr '25 – Mar '26) of $749K vs prior T12 (Apr '24 – Mar '25) of $728K, +2.9% YoY. The defensible 2026 forecast is $770K (+3.5% YoY), with the OM's $830K target remaining available as upside if the dynamic-pricing engine continues to compound. Either way, the value-driving story is NOI margin expansion — 30.3% → 36.8% → 40.9% on the forecast, with margin headroom to 44.6% at the OM target.
